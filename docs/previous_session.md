## What Changed

**The bento grid is content now, and image-ready.** Eleven tiles moved out of
`BentoGrid.tsx` into `dictionaries/{en,de}.ts`: the German homepage was showing English
labels (`ISSUE-005`), and an image is now a data edit (`ISSUE-004`). A tile with a `src`
renders it under a gradient scrim so its label stays legible; a tile without one stays the
grey card, and the two mix cleanly while the images are made one at a time.

**Every image slot on the site can be filled from data.** The 44 that had no source field
— 36 playground cards, the project image, the 8 About carousel slots, and the two hero
collage cards that were hard-coded in the page — all take `src`/`alt` now. `ISSUE-007` is
closed. `src/components/ui/Media.tsx` is the one component that chooses between the image
and the placeholder; `case-study/Figure.tsx` builds on it.

**`docs/reference/image_manifest.md`** — generated by `scripts/image-manifest.mjs` from
the data — lists **136 slots, 7 filled**, each with its aspect ratio, export width and
exact data path. It is the thing to work from when making images. Note that `src` lives in
both the `en` and `de` objects of the same file: one slot, two edits, translated `alt`.
